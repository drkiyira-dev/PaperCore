Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is this just a ChatGPT wrapper?

No. The salience ranking and rule engine run locally and do the core work. A large language model is optional and off by default — Paper Core works fully with it switched off.

How is my privacy protected?

Everything runs on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, and it works completely offline. You can read the source to confirm it.

What kinds of papers can it read?

Native and scanned PDFs (via on-device OCR), bilingual English / 中文 documents, long papers, and content with formulas, tables and figures.

Is it free and open source?

Yes. Paper Core is MIT-licensed and the full pipeline is on GitHub — nothing is hidden behind a service.

Does it replace reading the paper?

No — it surfaces the core and anchors every line back to the original passage, so you spend your reading time on what actually matters.
